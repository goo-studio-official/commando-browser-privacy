Privacy Policy

Commando Browser · Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Goo Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the Commando Browser Android app (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: your browsing history, bookmarks, downloads and settings stay on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not sell your personal information. The App shows ads and uses analytics, and those third-party services may collect limited device and usage data as described below.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

To work as a browser, the App stores the following locally on your phone. We do not receive this data on our servers.

  • Browsing history, open tabs, bookmarks and saved pages
  • Downloaded files and download history
  • App settings (theme, ad blocking, popup blocking, Do Not Track, Wi‑Fi only downloads, app lock, and similar)
  • Cookies, cache and site data created by the websites you visit

Incognito tabs do not save history, and cookies created in them are cleared when you close them. You can clear your browsing data at any time from Settings, or by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings. Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App includes third-party SDKs that may automatically collect information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from IP, app interactions, crash and performance data. Their use of this data is governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Permissions We Request

The App asks for permissions only when a feature needs them. You can change or revoke them in your phone’s settings.

  • Internet / network state: to load web pages, news and ads.
  • Location (approximate or precise): only if a website you visit asks for it and you allow it. The App does not track your location in the background.
  • Camera and microphone: only when a website asks to use them (for example video calls, voice search or QR scanning) and you allow it. We do not record or store this content.
  • Storage / files: to save and open files you download or upload.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show download progress and alerts.
  • Advertising ID: used by ad services as described above.

4. Websites and Content You Visit

When you open a website, search with a search engine, or read news inside the App, your device connects directly to that website or service. They may collect data under their own policies, which we do not control. The App’s news and sports sections load content from third-party sources such as Google News.

5. Feedback You Send Us

If you use the in-app Feedback form, we receive the message you write, any screenshots you attach, and the email address you choose to provide, so we can reply. This is delivered to our support email through a form-delivery service. We use it only to respond and to improve the App.

6. Privacy and Security Features

The App offers ad blocking, popup blocking, Do Not Track requests, incognito tabs and an optional app lock. These tools reduce tracking but cannot guarantee complete protection. Do Not Track is a request that websites may ignore.

7. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the App
  • To fix bugs and measure performance
  • To show ads that help keep the App free
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Sharing

We share data only with the service providers listed above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users’ safety. We do not share your local browsing history, bookmarks or downloads.

9. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Data held by third-party services follows their retention policies.
  • You may request access to or deletion of any personal data we hold (such as a feedback email) by contacting us below.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International Users

Third-party services may process data in countries other than your own. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, California or another region with privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Where required, ad services may ask for your consent.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no app or internet transmission is completely secure. Please keep your device updated and use the app lock if others have access to your phone.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
